# Filming a clip for M2 (Clip mode)

1. Phone on a stand at eye level, 50 to 80 cm away. Head, shoulders and chest in the picture.
2. Face a window or lamp. Keep still. Hold the phone steady and in landscape.
3. Film 60 to 70 seconds, once calm (this teaches M2 the calm baseline) and once after something mildly stressful, for example a hard mental-arithmetic question read aloud.
4. If a pulse oximeter is available, note its number at the same time. Show it only as a side note, not as proof of accuracy.
