M²

A calm voice beside you when a message has shaken you.

Talk to M² out loud. With your permission, a camera reads your breathing, pulse and face, so M² can tell when you are tense and help you slow down. M² is a wellness companion. It is not therapy and it never diagnoses.

  • Live mode never records or saves video, audio or your messages. Nothing is stored on disk.
  • Clip mode records about a minute of video into a temporary file, only so Presage can read it, and deletes it as soon as the reading ends. A clip you choose yourself is read where it is and is never copied or deleted. Audio and messages are never saved.
  • The camera is processed by the Presage SmartSpectra SDK, which keeps a session with Presage's servers while it runs. Its aggregate telemetry is switched off. See "Consent and data handling" in the README.
  • Anything M² says about your face or body is a gentle guess for you to correct.
